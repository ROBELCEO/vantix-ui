Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. What Data We Collect

Vantix requires access to your Shopify store to function. We collect the following data:

  • Store Metrics: Your product titles, prices, inventory levels, and Cost of Goods Sold (COGS).
  • Order Data: Order totals, line items, and timestamps (used to calculate Revenue and Velocity).
  • Account Data: Your email address (via Google OAuth) and Telegram Chat ID (if you connect alerts).
  • Competitor Data: Publicly available product titles, prices, and stock statuses from stores you explicitly choose to track.

2. Why We Collect This Data

We do not sell your data. We collect it exclusively to power your dashboard:

  • True Net Profit: We subtract your COGS, gateway fees, and shipping costs from your revenue.
  • Safe Floor: We calculate the exact lowest price you can offer before losing money.
  • AI Margin Advisor: We send anonymized product titles and pricing data to our AI provider (Groq) to generate strategic advice. We never send your personal identity or customer lists to the AI.

3. Data Security

All data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and stored securely via Supabase (PostgreSQL). Your Shopify Access Tokens are encrypted at rest and are never exposed to the frontend browser.

4. Data Deletion

You have the right to delete your account at any time. Going to Settings -> Danger Zone will permanently wipe your store connection, competitor lists, alerts, and personal data from our servers immediately.
