Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

At Midra, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal and store data.

1. Data We Collect

We collect Account Data (email address), Store Data (Shopify domain, product catalogs), and Competitor Data (publicly available prices).

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to calculate net profit, track competitor price drops, and generate AI-driven pricing strategies. We do not sell your personal data.

3. Third-Party Integrations

Midra connects with Shopify (for store data), Supabase (for authentication), and Groq/Gemini (for AI). We only send product titles and prices, never personal customer data.

4. Data Security

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We use Supabase's secure infrastructure to ensure your store data remains private.
